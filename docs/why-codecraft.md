# Why CodeCraft

## The problem

Running a small business means doing a lot of jobs at once. The owner is the
salesperson, the marketer, the person answering messages, and the strategist.
There is never enough time. Hiring a full team costs too much. Leads go
cold because follow-ups are slow. Customer messages pile up. Marketing posts
go out when there's a spare hour, which is rarely.

## What CodeCraft is

A team of digital assistants, organised like a small company — Sales,
Marketing, Support, Strategy, Development. Each one drafts the day-to-day
work. The owner reads what was drafted, taps **Approve**, and only then does
anything go out.

## Benefits by department

| Department | What it drafts | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Sales** | Lead lists from web search; personalised cold emails and WhatsApp messages; follow-ups on Day 4, 10 and 21 | More consistent outreach without carving out a sales afternoon |
| **Marketing** | Social posts and marketing copy; full content packages for events (caption, hashtags, cover image) | Regular on-brand posts without hiring an agency |
| **Support** | Inbox triage (drops autoresponders and your own emails); replies to messages that genuinely need one; replies to WhatsApp and Telegram as soon as they arrive; meeting bookings on a real calendar | Faster response times; fewer missed messages |
| **Strategy** | Co-founder-style strategy writing; cited research summaries; recurring daily or weekly briefings that call out what's new | Better decisions in less time |
| **Development** | Small coded files (a flyer, a landing page, a template); find or clean files on the owner's computer; summarise web pages | Removes small chores that would otherwise take a freelancer |

## Why it's different

- **Nothing goes out without approval.** Every send, every delete, every
  calendar invite becomes a Tasks-page card. The owner approves or rejects
  before anything leaves the system.
- **Scheduled work.** A workflow (a daily briefing, a weekly outreach batch)
  runs on a timer. The timer only runs while the server is on; the hosting
  plan the business chooses decides whether that's always or only during
  business hours.
- **Everything in one place.** One dashboard for email, WhatsApp, Telegram,
  research, content, and tasks waiting for approval.
- **Built for how local businesses work.** WhatsApp, Telegram, phone numbers,
  and email are all handled the same way.
- **Each business gets its own workspace** in the database. Isolation between
  workspaces is being finalised; the final enforcement step is on the path
  for the current release cycle.

## Who it's for

- A solo founder or small team doing all the roles themselves.
- A business that gets most of its enquiries by email, WhatsApp, or
  messaging apps.
- Someone comfortable reading a draft and tapping Approve before anything
  goes out.

## Who it's not for

- A business that wants fully automatic sending with no one in the loop
  (the approval step is deliberate and not optional).
- A regulated industry where every outbound message needs compliance review
  (CodeCraft doesn't do that review for you).
- A business whose customers only communicate in person or by voice calls.

## Common questions

**Will it send messages without me?**
No. Every email, every WhatsApp, every calendar invite is drafted and
queued. You tap Approve on the Tasks page before anything leaves the
system. There is an optional per-channel auto-reply mode for WhatsApp and
Telegram that is **off by default** — even when turned on it refuses to send
anything that mentions refunds, complaints, or placeholder text, and it is
limited to a small number of messages per day.

**Is my data private?**
Each business gets its own workspace in the database. The code checks every
read and write against the right workspace, and a health panel in the
dashboard shows the owner that nothing is slipping through. The final
enforcement step is in testing.

**Do I need technical skills?**
No. The owner never writes code. Someone with basic admin skills does the
one-time setup (connecting Gmail, WhatsApp, etc.). After that the owner uses
a chat window and a Tasks page.

**What if it drafts something wrong?**
You see every outbound message before it goes out. If a draft is off, click
**Reject** or edit the text before approving. If a research summary gets a
fact wrong, you'll see it when you read the output.

## Current limitations

- One admin per workspace in this release — adding a second admin is turned
  off until the next feature lands.
- Scheduled workflows only run while the server is on. On low-cost hosting
  plans the server sleeps after a period of inactivity; on paid plans it
  stays on.
- The WhatsApp side doesn't yet read voice notes or photo messages.
- Database storage limits of the hosting plan apply (free tiers are
  tight).
