What goes in the dough

Flour, Water, Time

01

Flour

Stone-milled at a family gristmill two counties over, whole grain and never bleached. We rotate in einkorn, spelt, and rye so the board changes with what the mill has that week — the bread tastes like the harvest, not a formula.

02

Water

Filtered and brought to the exact temperature the dough needs, down to the degree, because the starter reacts to warmth the way it reacts to nothing else. Hydration runs high — 78 to 85 percent — for that open, glassy crumb.

03

Time

No commercial yeast ever touches this kitchen. Our starter, Bertha, has been fed twice daily since 2014. Fermentation runs 24 to 72 hours in the walk-in — slow enough for the wild yeast and bacteria to do the actual work.
