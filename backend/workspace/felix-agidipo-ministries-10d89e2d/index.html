Who we are

A family gathered around one flame.

For over two decades, Firstlight Assembly has been a gathering place for the neighborhood and beyond — rooted in scripture, carried by worship, and devoted to practical love. We're not interested in performance. We're interested in presence: God's, and each other's.

Whether you're taking your first step through our doors or you've sat in the same pew for years, there's a seat with your name on it.
