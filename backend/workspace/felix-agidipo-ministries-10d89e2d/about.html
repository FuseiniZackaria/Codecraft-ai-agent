Who We Are

A house built on light, family and the gospel

Since 2005, Zion Gate Ministries has stood as a gathering place for the seeking, the weary, and the faithful — carrying the light break of Christ into everyday life across our city and beyond.

Our Founders

Called to open a gate, not build a fortress

Pastor Emmanuel Adeyemi began Zion Gate Ministries in 2005 with fourteen members meeting in a rented hall, convinced that the church should be a gate people walk through into freedom — not a fortress that keeps them out. Alongside his wife, Pastor Grace, he has shepherded a congregation now spanning three services, a Bible institute, and outreach arms across five communities.

Their ministry is marked by expository preaching, deliberate discipleship, and a conviction that worship should be as excellent as it is sincere — a golden thread of light running through every gathering, every sermon, every act of service.

Our Journey

Twenty years, gate by gate

  1. 2005

    The first gathering

    Fourteen believers meet in a rented community hall in Ilupeju, committing to prayer, the Word, and one another.

  2. 2009

    A permanent home

    Zion Gate breaks ground on its first sanctuary, funded entirely by the sacrificial giving of its members.

  3. 2013

    The Bible Institute opens

    A two-year discipleship and leadership training school launches, raising up teachers, deacons and future pastors.

  4. 2017

    Outreach expands

    Feeding programs, a widows' fund, and a prison ministry begin, extending the gate into the wider community.

  5. 2020

    Going digital

    Weekly services move online during global lockdowns, growing an international congregation that remains with us today.

  6. 2025

    Twenty years of light

    Now three campuses strong, Zion Gate continues to open its doors to the seeking and the searching, week after week.

Our Vision

To be a gate of light through which every generation passes into the fullness of life found in Jesus Christ — raising a family of believers who carry that light into their homes, workplaces and nations.

✦

Our Mission

We exist to preach the uncompromised Word, disciple believers into maturity, worship with excellence, and serve our community tangibly — so that no one who comes near Zion Gate leaves the same way they came.

What Anchors Us

Our Core Values

01

The Word, Unfiltered

We hold Scripture as final authority, taught in full context and without dilution — the light by which every other value is measured.

02

Family First

The church is a household. We build genuine relationships, not just attendance figures.

03

Excellence in Worship

What we offer to God — in music, service and presentation — deserves our very best effort.

04

Radical Service

Faith without works is dead. We serve our city with our hands, not just our prayers.

05

Integrity in the Dark

Who we are in private — in finances, in character, in conduct — must match who we present in public.

06

Every Generation

From cradle roll to eldership, we design ministry that meets every age exactly where they are.
