Our Founder & Servant-Leader

A Life Given
to the Light

For over two decades, Rev. Felix Agidipo has carried one conviction into villages, city halls, prisons, and sanctuaries alike: that the gospel is not a message to be defended but a light to be released.

The Journey

From a Kerosene Lamp
to a Thousand Pulpits

1994

A Boy, a Bible, a Blackout

Born in Ilesa, Nigeria, Felix Agidipo first read Scripture aloud by kerosene lamp during nightly power cuts — reciting Psalms to younger siblings as thunder rolled outside. It was in that flickering, imperfect light that he first understood: God does not wait for perfect conditions to shine.

2003

The Call in the Quarry

Working as a stone-quarry laborer to fund his studies, a near-fatal rockslide left him the sole survivor of his crew. He walked away with a shattered wrist and an unshakeable sense of commission — he preached his first open-air sermon from a pile of rubble two weeks later.

2009

Seminary & the Silent Years

Formal training at the West Africa Theological Seminary sharpened his exegesis, but it was three quiet years pastoring a twelve-member congregation in a disused market stall that taught him patience is its own form of ministry.

2016

Founding the Ministry

With eleven founding members and one borrowed generator, Rev. Agidipo established this ministry as a home for the overlooked — day laborers, market women, the formerly incarcerated, and the quietly grieving.

Today

A Growing Sunrise

The ministry now spans weekly gatherings, prison outreach, a widows' relief fund, and a broadcast reach across three states — still rooted in the same conviction that carried a boy through the dark: the light always breaks through.

Vision

A dawn that no darkness can hold back.

We envision communities — from the smallest rural chapel to the most overlooked city corner — awakened to the reality that grace is already breaking through their circumstance, waiting only to be named and received.

Mission

To carry gospel light into every shadowed room.

Through preaching, discipleship, mercy ministry, and honest presence in hard places, we exist to make the good news tangible — in prisons and boardrooms, hospital wards and market squares — until every person has stood in its warmth at least once.

Core Values

What Steadies the Flame

Radiant Truth

Scripture taught plainly, without flattery or fear — because light that is dimmed to be comfortable stops being light.

Costly Compassion

Mercy that shows up — in bail money, hospital visits, and shared meals — not mercy that only speaks.

Unshaken Integrity

Financial transparency, personal accountability, and a leadership team that answers to more than applause.

Joyful Endurance

A ministry born in hardship that refuses bitterness — choosing gladness as an act of defiant faith.

Wide Welcome

No one is too far gone, too poor, too broken, or too polished to be handed a seat at this table.

Generational Sight

We plant what we may not harvest — raising disciples, leaders, and institutions built to outlast us.

"I have never once preached to convince a room. I preach to remind it — that the sun rose this morning whether or not anyone in the dark believed it would." — Rev. Felix Agidipo
