01 — The Ministry

A Voice for the
Overlooked and Overwhelmed

For over two decades, Rev. Felix Agidipo has ministered at the intersection of scripture and street — bringing gospel clarity into hospital wards, prison yards, campuses, and Sunday sanctuaries alike. His teaching is plain-spoken, deeply rooted in Word, and unafraid of the questions people actually carry.

Rev. Agidipo Ministries exists to make that light portable: recorded sermons for the sleepless night, outreach for the forgotten neighborhood, and a community that keeps showing up when it matters most.
